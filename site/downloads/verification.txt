Each shock response is measured against its own prior-specific baseline. Changes in responses do not alone reveal differences in outcome levels. The result is a conditional state comparison under the supplied solution, not a stationary average or a claim about welfare. It does not by itself identify the economic mechanism behind nonmonotonicity.
Belief is the model probability of committed type, not an observed narrative credibility score. The actual type is held strategic; beliefs evolve after initialization through the original Bayesian update. Common future income and implementation draws are retained. No equilibrium or parameter is changed.
Monte Carlo SEs are conditional simulation error, not empirical confidence intervals or parameter uncertainty. Fiscal adjustment y-c cannot distinguish taxes and spending.
